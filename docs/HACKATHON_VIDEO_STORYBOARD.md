## 🎥 Production Notes

### Recording Setup
- **Screen Recording:** Use OBS or similar for 1080p 60fps gameplay
- **Audio:** Clear voiceover or on-screen captions (captions recommended for accessibility)
- **Music:** Light detective/mystery theme music (low volume, royalty-free)

### Key Highlights to Emphasize
1. **Hybrid Architecture** - GameMaker + Devvit integration
2. **Daily Engagement** - Streak system and UTC-based reset
3. **Polish** - Premium UI, animations, typewriter effects
4. **Community** - Share feature, leaderboards, ranks
5. **Technical Quality** - Tests, TypeScript, production-ready

### Recording Checklist
- [ ] Record fresh Daily Case gameplay (win scenario)
- [ ] Record Archives gameplay
- [ ] Capture streak counter incrementing
- [ ] Show leaderboard with real data
- [ ] Record share-to-Reddit flow
- [ ] Screen record code/architecture (5-10 seconds max)
- [ ] Export at 1080p MP4

### Recommended Tools
- **Screen Recording:** OBS Studio, QuickTime (Mac), or Game Bar (Windows)
- **Video Editing:** DaVinci Resolve (free), iMovie, or Premiere Pro
- **Music:** YouTube Audio Library, Epidemic Sound
- **Captions:** Kapwing, Rev.com, or YouTube auto-captions
